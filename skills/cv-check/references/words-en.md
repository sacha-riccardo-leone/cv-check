# English: words and phrases to flag

These are not rejected by software. They cost attention: recruiters read them hundreds of times a day, and many now read them as AI-written. The fix is almost always the same: **replace the claim with the proof** (what you did, for whom, with what result).

## Empty self-descriptions (show it, don't say it)
results-driven · detail-oriented · team player · hard-working / hardworking · self-starter · go-getter · motivated · highly motivated · dynamic · passionate (about) · enthusiastic · dedicated · reliable · proactive · strategic thinker · problem solver · creative thinker · people person · excellent communication skills · strong work ethic · fast learner / quick learner · thinks outside the box · goes above and beyond · wears many hats · rockstar / ninja / guru

## Inflated or worn-out verbs (fine once; flag when repeated or vague)
spearheaded · leveraged · utilized (→ used) · orchestrated · championed · synergized · revolutionized · transformed (with no result) · helped / assisted with (→ say what *you* did) · responsible for / duties included (→ action + result) · worked on · was involved in · participated in

## AI tells (very common in generated text)
delve · tapestry · testament to · navigate the complexities · in today's fast-paced world · ever-evolving landscape · robust · seamless(ly) · cutting-edge · pivotal · meticulous(ly) · elevate · foster · harness · unlock · empower · realm · journey (for a career) · passionate about leveraging · a proven track record of · I thrive in · eager to contribute · aligns perfectly with · I am confident that my skills · em-dash chains — like this — everywhere · every bullet in groups of three

## Filler
proven track record · fast-paced environment · various / numerous / multiple (→ the number) · etc. · successfully (→ the result shows it) · in order to (→ to) · references available upon request · objective: to obtain a challenging position

## Cover letter openers and closers
I am writing to express my interest in… · I am excited to apply for… · Please find attached my resume · I believe I would be a great fit · Thank you for your time and consideration (fine once, don't stack it with three other closers) · To whom it may concern (→ the hiring manager's name, or "Dear Hiring Team")

## Risky content
- Salary history, reasons for leaving, "fired", health, religion, politics: cut unless asked.
- Photo, age, marital status (US/UK/CA): cut.
- Unexplained internal jargon or project codenames: replace with what it was.
